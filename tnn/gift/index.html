A quiet place for the two of you, right in the middle of a full and busy life.

Then, Now, Next is a guided journal for couples. It asks for nothing but an evening and each other.

Buy on Amazon

Paperback · 7 × 10 in · 136 pages

Write apart. Take turns writing on your own pages, without seeing each other’s answers.

Then read. When both pages are done, read each other’s words.

Then talk. Ask about what surprised you, then fill in the page you share.

When the last page is done, you have your story in your own words, written twice, once in each hand.

A gift for a wedding or an engagement, an anniversary, Valentine’s Day, Christmas, or for the two of you, with no reason at all.
